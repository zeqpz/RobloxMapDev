# Southwest city style (Albuquerque + El Paso)

Reference set: `research/city-reference/REPORT.html` (open via a local server; the images won't load from
file://), images in `research/city-reference/shots/<abq|ep>/<category>/`, metadata + Google Maps links in
`research/city-reference/data/shots.jsonl`. Google imagery is **reference only** - never use it as a texture.

## Big picture
- Low and wide: mostly one-story buildings on big lots behind walls and fences, on 6-lane arterials; one tower
  cluster downtown. Realism comes from ground-level detail: walls, gates, bars, signs, poles, wires.
- **Albuquerque = stucco:** flat-roof boxes with stepped parapets and *canales*, sand/tan/salmon/white, coyote
  (latilla) fences, CMU block walls, gravel yards, bare cottonwoods, the Sandia Mountains behind everything.
- **El Paso = brick + rock:** buff and red brick, river-rock walls everywhere (yards, arterial walls, buttressed
  retaining walls), painted block shops with hand-lettered signs, fan palms + mesquite, the Franklin Mountains.

## Building types (with typical studs - check sizes.json)
- Flat-roof stucco house: 1 story, wall top 11 + parapet 2; barred aluminum slider windows; iron screen door;
  **rooftop swamp cooler** boxes (white/grey, ~3.5 studs cube) and vents; satellite dish.
- Ranch house: low 4:12 shingle roof, attached garage/carport, brick wainscot (EP) or stucco (ABQ).
- Fourplex (ABQ War Zone): 2 stories, stair tower with a diamond/arch motif, gravel lot, iron fence.
- Brick tenement (EP Segundo Barrio): 2 stories, corbelled brick cornice, iron balconies, door grilles, dirt alley
  behind (16-20 studs).
- Strip mall: arcade (square columns or arches) 11-14 deep, parapet sign band, roll-up shutters, red fire curb.
- Corner store / taqueria: painted block in loud colors, hand-lettered signs, barred windows, ice box out front.
- Route 66 motel: 2-story single-loaded, exterior walkway + rails, neon pole sign.
- Gas station: flat canopy (18 clearance) with colored fascia, store box, raised gravel/boulder islands, price pylon.
- Big box: tan split-face block, entry vestibule, cart corrals, loading dock.

## Hardware, walls, street
- Iron bars on windows/doors, iron fences between stucco or rock pillars, chain-link (barbed top), roll-up shutters.
- Wooden utility poles with crossarms, transformers and sagging wires along hood streets and alleys.
- Galvanized mast-arm signals (ABQ black heads; EP yellow heads with backplates), green street blades, cobra-head
  lights; continental crosswalks; cracked, patched, sun-faded asphalt; concrete-lined drainage channels.

## Plants + terrain
Cottonwood, Siberian elm, juniper, chamisa, yucca, cholla, gravel xeriscape (ABQ); fan palm, mesquite, desert willow,
creosote, oleander (EP). Ground is gravel and tan dirt; lawns only in parks/golf/bosque. Clear hard sunlight, pale sky.

## Brands
Keep the read (canopy colors, striped A-frame burger stand, tan big box) but use fictional names and logos only.
